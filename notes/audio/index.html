Listen before building

Companion examples for Designing Musical Networks, rerun with weft v0.2.0. Start with the recording, then compare the attack, sustained partials, breath/bow noise, and release. The cello includes a transform-only reference and a direct per-note fit.

Copies in each recording group have matched whole-clip RMS and a common peak constraint. This does not equalize perceived loudness. The VAE application already normalizes and fades its output; its original processing has not been undone. Training and raw-output measurements are documented in the experiment protocol.
